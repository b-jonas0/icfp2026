About

This is the 29th instance of the annual ICFP Programming Contest, held online. It runs from Friday July 24th at 12:00 UTC to Monday July 27th at 12:00 UTC, with a 24-hour lightning round ending Saturday July 25th at 12:00 UTC. Winners will be announced at ICFP 2026 in Indianapolis, Indiana.
